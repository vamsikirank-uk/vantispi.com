2. What we collect

Contact form. If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address, product of interest and message you type. We use them only to reply to you. Technical data. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies or trackers on this site. The site stores one setting in your browser's local storage, your light or dark theme choice, which stays on your device and is not sent to us.

We process enquiries on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to you, or to take steps at your request before a contract. We keep enquiries for as long as needed to deal with them and for up to two years afterwards, unless a longer period is required by law.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the service providers named above that deliver the form and host the site, and where the law requires.

Under UK data protection law you may ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and may object to or ask us to restrict our use of it. Email contact@vantispi.com. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
